Hoqqa is a story-based platformer, following a journey of joy, regret and friendship. You take the role of Hoqqa, a naive and hasty magician, tasked with bringing joy. When the game of light fails, the shadow teaches mercilessly.

Be aware that Hoqqa is a prototype. No warranty is given on the game's uninterrupted operation or freedom from errors.

Hoqqa is a video game produced for academic research purposes within the scope of an MFA thesis titled Emotion Transmission in the Context of Digital Game Design and a 2D Platformer Game Implementation, conducted at Hacettepe University, Institute of Fine Arts, Department of Graphic Design.

AI was used for the music and to assist with some of the complex coding in this game. The rest is handcrafted.
