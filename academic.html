Short Biography

Batuhan Dikmen is a researcher, graphic artist and designer. He is currently working as a research assistant at Başkent University, Department of Graphic Design and pursuing his Doctorate in Arts at Hacettepe University, Department of Graphic Design. He has nearly 4 years of professional experience in the video game industry as a 2D Game Artist and holds an MFA degree with a thesis on video game graphics. His work aims to bridge the gap between academic theory and practical design.
